Duvet

by Bôa

Description

Released in 1998, Bôa's Duvet is the opening track on Bôa's album Twilight.

It is best known for its use as the theme song for the cult-classic anime Serial Experiments Lain, which features surreal and avant-garde imagery and explores philosophical topics such as reality, identity, and communication.

In August 2021, the song saw a massive resurgence in popularity, especially on TikTok.
